# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for
everyone who participates — contributors, maintainers, and users alike — regardless of background or
identity.

## Our standard

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as our code of conduct. Please read it in full at that link. In short: be respectful and constructive,
assume good faith, give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Behaviour that is disrespectful, harassing, or otherwise harmful is not acceptable in any project space.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official channel)
and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer at
**andrew@dataweb.co.za**. Reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly and in good faith. As a
solo-maintained project, responses are best-effort.
