mascot-forge: before / after

One pipeline, two visually different assets — a pixel-art creature and a cartoon vehicle — each shown baseline (left) vs forged (right). The forged panels have semantic parts that articulate independently and a state machine reacting to one live (mock) telemetry feed, auto-cycling idle → active → alert. Same engine, zero per-asset render code.

DevBrain — pixel-art creature (origin asset)

Before — flipbook baseline

Whole-sprite pre-rendered PNG. Motion is a flipbook of full-frame poses — the legs and antenna cannot move independently, and it knows nothing about live data.

After — forged & data-reactive

Independent part articulation + reacts to live data via the Phase-4 orchestrator. Priority alert > active > idle; downgrades hold for hysteresis.
